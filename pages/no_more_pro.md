# No More Pro Account

Panoptikum no longer has a paid Pro Account. All the features that used to require a Pro Account
are now available to every registered podcaster, free of charge:

* Change the URL of your persona page
* Change the persona image
* Change the persona description
* Redirect a persona to another persona
* Delegate a persona to another persona

There is nothing left to pay, nothing to trial and no payment reference to keep track of. Just log
in and use these functions directly from your profile.

See the [Podcaster Manual](/podcaster-manual) for details on how to claim and manage your persona.
